### Thinking about treating someone you love to some DJ lessions?

Great idea, they'll love it! Gifting someone some DJ lessions gives them a surprising and unique new experience, and is suitable for all ages, genders, and abilities.

Each gift card is valid for one hour, or 90 minutes when used as a first session for a new student. Each card has a unique code and each session can be booked independently at a later date by your recipient by using the codes on each card.

## {{< icon name="hand-point-right" >}} [**BUY GIFT VOUCHERS**](giftvouchers)

### Received a gift card?

If you're the lucky recipient of a Brighton DJ Tuition gift voucher, just use our online booking system, choose the intro or one-hour option, and enter your card's voucher code at the checkout to skip payment and get your session booked.

For two-hour sessions: _Due to a limitation in our booking system, you can't use a one-hour gift card code when booking a two-hour session. If you have multiple one-hour gift cards and want to combine two of them to book a two-hour session, please get in touch with us to exchange them for a two-hour session voucher code!_
